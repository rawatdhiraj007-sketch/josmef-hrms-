# 📧 Outbound Email Templates

Use these as STARTING POINTS — personalize each one. Mass-blast emails get marked as spam.

---

```
included (SSS R-3, BIR 2316, etc.), and tracks 30+ license types with automatic
expiry alerts.

If this resonates, I'd love to show you a 20-minute demo. Free 14-day trial after.

One-pager attached.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Workforce] · Manila, Philippines
[your.email] · [phone]

P.S. We're already speaking with [other hospital name] about their nursing
workforce — would love to compare notes on the PH market.
```

## ⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

- ❌ "We're the best HR system" → boring + unprovable. Say WHAT problem you solve instead.
- ❌ Long sales decks attached in first email
- ❌ Sending more than 1 follow-up per week
- ❌ Sounding desperate
- ❌ Not researching the org before emailing
- ❌ Generic "Dear Hiring Manager" greetings
- ❌ Forgetting to attach the one-pager
- ❌ Not having calendly / scheduling link ready

## ✅ What works

- ✅ Reference ONE specific thing about them
- ✅ Ask ONE question
- ✅ One CTA ("20-minute demo")
- ✅ Short — under 200 words
- ✅ Personal sign-off
- ✅ Attach the one-pager PDF
- ✅ Send Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am or 2-4pm
- ✅ Follow up exactly 4 days later if no reply, then 7 days, then leave it
