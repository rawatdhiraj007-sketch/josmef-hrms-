## 📋 Outbound Strategy

### Week 1: Top 10 hospitals (Tier 1)
- LinkedIn outreach to CHRO / VP HR
- Cold email with one-pager attached
- Goal: 2 demos booked

### Week 2: Multi-branch clinics (Tier 3) + home-care (Tier 4)
- Easiest sale — they FEEL the pain daily
- Goal: 3 demos booked

### Week 3-4: Provincial hospitals (Tier 2)
- Less competition, faster decision cycle
- Goal: First paid client signed

### Month 2+: Industrial clinics (Tier 5)
- Long sales cycle but big AOV
- Treat as "phase 2" pipeline

## 💬 Who to contact at each org

In order of preference:

1. **Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)** — best for hospitals
2. **VP / Head of HR** — most mid-size
3. **HR Director** — corporate clinics
4. **Operations Director / COO** — multi-branch clinics
5. **Owner / Medical Director** — small private clinics, dental

### Email subject lines that work
- `Quick question about [their clinic name]'s PRC renewals`
- `Saw you're hiring nurses — could save you 5 hours/week`
- `[Mutual contact / referral] suggested I reach out`
- `Healthcare HR built in PH — 20-min demo?`

### What NOT to do
- ❌ Mass email blast
- ❌ Lead with "Our software does X, Y, Z"
- ❌ Talk pricing in first email
- ❌ Send long sales decks unsolicited

### What TO do
- ✅ Research one specific thing about them
- ✅ Reference a specific pain (license expiry, scheduling chaos)
- ✅ Attach one-pager PDF
- ✅ Ask for 20 minutes (low commitment)
- ✅ Follow up 3-4 times spaced out

## ⚠️ Reality check

- **Hit rate on cold email:** 5-15% reply
- **Reply → demo:** 30-50%
- **Demo → trial:** 40-60%
- **Trial → paid:** 30-50%

**Math: 100 emails → 10 replies → 4 demos → 2 trials → 1 paying client**

Plan to send 200-400 personalized emails to land your first 2-4 paying clients in months 1-3.

## 🚫 Skip these for now

- ❌ **Public DOH hospitals** — gov procurement = 12-18 months
- ❌ **St. Luke's / Makati Medical** — already have enterprise systems
- ❌ **PGH** — public, slow procurement
- ❌ **Small single-doctor clinics** — under $200/mo doesn't justify your time

Focus on mid-size private healthcare with 30-500 employees. Sweet spot.
